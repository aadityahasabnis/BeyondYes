# BeyondYes

BeyondYes is a relationship intelligence platform that helps people understand themselves and understand each other before making lifelong relationship decisions.

## Mission

Help people build healthier relationships through self-awareness, communication, and intentional growth.

## Vision

Better marriages don't begin with better matches.

They begin with better understanding.

## Core Principles

- No matchmaking
- No personality labels
- No "pass or fail"
- Growth over judgment
- Evidence-informed assessments
- AI explains, not decides

## Features

### Self Assessment

- Emotional Intelligence
- Communication
- Conflict Style
- Values
- Financial Mindset
- Growth Mindset
- Relationship Readiness

### Couple Assessment

- Partner Invitations
- Independent Responses
- Compatibility Reports
- AI Insights
- Discussion Guides
- Shared Dashboard

### AI

- Individual Reports
- Compatibility Summaries
- Growth Suggestions
- Conversation Prompts
